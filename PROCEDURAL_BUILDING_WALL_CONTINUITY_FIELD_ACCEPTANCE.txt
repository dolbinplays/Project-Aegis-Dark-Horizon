Build: v0.26.09.15.1050_PROCEDURAL_BUILDING_WALL_CONTINUITY_HOTFIX_PATCH
Save format: 4

Purpose
Confirm the Browser 1050 presentation hotfix restores continuous intact procedural building walls after the Browser 0904 tetromino rollout without closing intentional openings or changing gameplay authority.

1. Generate representative buildings
- Start several fresh tactical maps until you can inspect a rectangular/O building and representative T, L/J, S/Z shapes.
- Include at least one shape with a clearly visible concave outdoor recess.
- Discover the complete building perimeter before judging continuity; hidden building shell rules should remain unchanged.

2. Inspect continuous intact walls
- In 3D Iso, inspect long straight wall runs from near and oblique angles.
- Inspect every visible footprint turn. A turn should read as two connected wall faces, not a thin pillar, isolated panel, or open vertical slit.
- Repeat in FPV and TPV where the facade is visible.
- If the fallback renderer can be selected/triggered in the current build, compare it with the persistent renderer. Both should present the same wall continuity.

3. Preserve intentional openings
- Doors must remain open and traversable.
- Window apertures must remain visibly open; the hotfix must not add a solid corner return to a window cell.
- Concave T/L/J/S/Z outdoor recess cells must remain outdoor/traversable rather than being bridged by a diagonal seam.
- Reveal or create a breach. The breached cell must remain open and must not retain a full-height corner return.

4. Verify gameplay authority still matches presentation
- Route a soldier beside and around intact wall turns; they must not walk through intact structural wall cells.
- Check LOS across an intact solid wall/turn and through a legitimate window/door/breach.
- Confirm cover/targeting behavior still follows the structural cover state rather than the presentation-only return mesh.

5. Save/reload and renderer/view changes
- Save during a shaped tactical mission, reload it, and reinspect the same wall turns and openings.
- Switch among 3D Iso/FPV/TPV and any available renderer/performance mode. Wall continuity should not require an unrelated camera or cover-state change to appear.
- Restore a pre-0904 in-progress rectangular tactical save if available; its legacy rectangular geometry should remain unchanged.

6. Regression reporting
If any gap remains, record the mission name/seed if visible, building family/rotation if known, exact view (3D Iso/FPV/TPV), and whether the gap is on a straight run, convex turn, concave turn, window, door, or breach. A screenshot from an oblique angle is especially useful.

Pass condition
Intact discovered facades read as continuous walls on both renderer paths, intentional openings/recesses stay open, save/reload preserves the same result, and movement/LOS/cover authority agrees with the structural building state.
