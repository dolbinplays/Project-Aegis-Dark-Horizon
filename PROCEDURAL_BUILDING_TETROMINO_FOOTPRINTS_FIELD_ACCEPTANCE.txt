PROJECT AEGIS — TETROMINO PROCEDURAL BUILDING FOOTPRINTS FIELD ACCEPTANCE
Build: v0.26.09.15.0904_TETROMINO_PROCEDURAL_BUILDING_FOOTPRINTS_PATCH
Save format: 4

Purpose
Verify that procedural tactical buildings use their actual I/O/T/L/J/S/Z footprint as the shared authority for presentation and gameplay rather than filling a rectangular bounding box.

1. Family / rotation coverage
- Generate several city, small-town, farm and nature tactical maps.
- Confirm examples of I, O, T, L, J, S and Z buildings appear over multiple seeds.
- Confirm rotated variants remain connected and retain sensible overall building scale.

2. Concave footprint integrity
- Inspect T/L/J/S/Z recesses from 3D Iso and perspective views.
- Confirm recessed cells remain outdoors: no phantom wall, floor, roof or interior furnishing should occupy the empty notch.
- Walk a unit through an open recess where terrain/pathing permits. It must not be treated as an interior or blocked building cell.

3. Walls, windows and doors
- Inspect convex and concave corners for continuous perimeter walls without duplicate slabs or unexplained gaps.
- Confirm windows only replace valid perimeter wall cells.
- Confirm every generated door is on the actual perimeter and opens toward a usable connected interior cell.
- Breach/window visibility and discovered-wall presentation should behave as they did before this patch.

4. Roofs and cutaway
- Confirm roof panels cover only occupied footprint cells and do not bridge T/L/J/S/Z recesses.
- Select/move AEGIS soldiers inside structures and verify roof cutaway still follows known AEGIS occupancy/camera occlusion.
- Confirm roof presentation returns correctly when the building is no longer cut away.

5. Interiors / AI / civilians
- Confirm furnishings and power controls appear only inside actual footprint cells.
- Confirm generated civilians/VIPs do not start in empty concave recesses.
- Confirm AI units can enter/exit through valid openings and building egress does not target a phantom rectangular edge.

6. Save/reload compatibility
- Save a newly generated tetromino battle, reload it and verify the same family, rotation, walls, doors, roof outline and interior occupancy remain aligned.
- Load a pre-patch live tactical save containing rectangular procedural buildings. That already-started battle should retain its legacy rectangular building geometry instead of being reinterpreted as a tetromino map.
- Finish either battle and confirm campaign save/load remains normal. Save format must remain 4.
