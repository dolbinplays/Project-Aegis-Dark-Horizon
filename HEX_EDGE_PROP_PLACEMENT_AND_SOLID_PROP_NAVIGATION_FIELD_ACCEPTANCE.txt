Project Aegis — Hex-Edge Prop Placement & Solid Prop Navigation Field Acceptance

Scope
- Qualifying one-cell terrain props render toward a deterministic edge of their owning hex instead of on the unit center point.
- Props that visually represent solid physical objects participate in the same authoritative movement blocker set used by manual movement, AI movement, spawn/open-cell checks, civilians/VIPs, and aliens.
- Low foliage remains passable unless another system explicitly marks it hard.

Acceptance
1. Trees, lamp posts, traffic lights, stop signs, vending/news machines, bus stops, benches, rocks, concrete, crates, wrecks, fences, and hay classify as solid navigation props while alive.
2. Bushes, brush, and crops remain passable soft terrain.
3. Destroyed solid props stop blocking movement.
4. Closed unlocked building doors remain traversable/auto-open capable; locked doors remain blockers.
5. Generated qualifying one-cell props carry deterministic propPlacementMode, propEdgeDirectionKey, and propEdgeFraction metadata.
6. Edge fractions remain inside the owning hex (< 0.5 center-to-neighbor distance), with slimmer poles/signs placed nearest the edge.
7. Curb fixtures prefer road/lane/path-facing edges when legal; natural props avoid road-facing edges when alternatives exist.
8. Building ingress protected cells, neighboring occupied cover cells, and building cells are avoided when choosing a visual edge.
9. Multihex vehicles, civic landmarks, building structural pieces, and interior furniture retain their existing centroid/footprint placement.
10. Three.js cover anchoring consumes the shared saved edge-placement metadata so 3D Iso / FPV / TPV agree.
11. Navigation classification does not change LOS semantics by itself; existing cover/visibility authority remains responsible for LOS.
12. Campaign save format remains 4.
