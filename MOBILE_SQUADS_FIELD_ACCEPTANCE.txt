MOBILE SQUADS — PHYSICAL DEVICE ACCEPTANCE
Build: v0.26.09.08.2242_MOBILE_SQUADS_ADAPTIVE_LAYOUT_PATCH

Use a disposable campaign on actual phones/tablets and in an installed PWA.

1. Choose Mobile · Adaptive, open Squads, and confirm both command rails remain visible. Hold the phone landscape and switch among Members, Assign, and Command.
2. Select soldiers and scroll the compact list and dossier independently. Open dossier only, stats only, and both; each choice should carry to another soldier until explicitly changed.
3. Use direct Add/Remove buttons. The soldier should move between the correct lists, counts should update, and a selected soldier's stale actions should close. Fill a squad and confirm another assignment is blocked by the existing capacity rule.
4. Upgrade capacity in Command, then assign the newly permitted member. Check chain of command, squad power, legacy, recruitment, and the selected-base control remain reachable by scrolling.
5. With squads at two bases, switch the squad selector. Assign should show only eligible, unassigned soldiers at that squad's station. Wounded, overflow, KIA, in-transit, and other-base personnel must stay excluded. The other-base count should remain accurate if the command base selector changes.
6. Try each soldier sort option and confirm comparison values and order agree. Use a long name/callsign and a large available roster.
7. Rotate portrait, open a soldier, and use Back to list. The list should replace the dossier and return focus to that soldier. Rotate landscape again and confirm both panes fit.
8. Check empty squads/rosters and recruitment arrival information. If available, inspect an existing force-assigned wounded member without changing the Sickbay override rules.
9. Switch to Standard through Menu / Save. Confirm the original squad overview, leadership/legacy, and full member/available card lists return; switch back to Mobile.
10. Repeat with browser chrome expanded/collapsed and on a tablet. Record device, OS, browser/PWA mode, orientation, and any clipped controls, horizontal scrolling, or touch/focus problems.
