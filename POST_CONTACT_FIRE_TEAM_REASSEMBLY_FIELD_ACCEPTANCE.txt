Save format: 4

1. NORMAL REGROUP
- Use a 3- or 4-soldier fire team and let members spread during a real alien firefight.
- Resolve the visible/Last Known Contact threat.
- Confirm the effective leader stops advancing while out-of-position supports route back into formation.
- Confirm the team resumes its previous Default Search or explicit mission assignment only after recovery.

2. LEADER CASUALTY / SUCCESSION
- Repeat while the original fire-team leader is killed, downed, extracted or otherwise removed during the fight.
- Confirm normal fire-team reconciliation selects a surviving effective leader.
- Confirm the team still enters REFORM rather than forgetting the pending regroup and scattering.

3. UNREACHABLE SUPPORT
- Create a legal scenario where one support cannot route to its formation slot while the other reachable members can.
- Confirm the reachable members attempt recovery first.
- After three recovery rounds, confirm the unreachable member does not deadlock the whole team once every reachable support is formed.
- Confirm the separated member still follows normal formation catch-up later if a path becomes available.

4. PRIORITY EXCEPTIONS
- Visible alien combat and unresolved Last Known Contact must interrupt/hold recovery.
- Active VIP/civilian escort and casualty duties must not be broken just to satisfy formation.
- Beacon Assault forward-reform/breacher behavior must remain intact.
- Hybrid player orders and explicit VIP/Beacon objective ownership must survive the recovery transition.

5. SAVE / PLAYBACK
- Save/reload during or immediately after the fight and verify the pending team regroup is preserved.
- Check streamed playback/status: members should not appear to branch into unrelated objectives before recovery is complete.
- Confirm save format remains 4.
