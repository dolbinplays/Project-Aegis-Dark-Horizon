Authoritative objective order

Contact interruption
- Let one free soldier/fire team begin exploration or VIP-approach movement, then expose a new alien partway through the route.
- The stale movement must stop at the first sighting cell; all remaining free fire-team actions must recalculate toward visible contact.
- Existing escort leaders remain on their escort.
- Civilian Escort Support = Engage Spotted Aliens: support soldiers break off to fight.
- Civilian Escort Support = Stay With Escort: supports remain with the escort.
- Civilian Escort Support = Ask When Contact Is Spotted: supports remain held until the player decides.

VIP/civilian priority
- With no visible/Last Known alien and no higher source objective, expose a tracker VIP or ordinary civilian.
- A free fire-team leader should receive the known civilian/VIP assignment and route toward that location.
- Free teams must not perform generic exploration while a known unassigned civilian/VIP still exists.
- Once every known civilian/VIP is assigned/escorted/resolved, lower-priority exploration may resume.

Beacon / UFO ordering
- With no visible/Last Known contact or active escort conflict, confirm an active Field Beacon and an unchecked crashed-UFO bay.
- Beacon responsibility must be allocated before UFO-bay inspection.
- Known unassigned civilian approach must not steal the fire-team reserved for the higher-priority source objective.

Post-contact cohesion
- Spread a fire team naturally in combat, then clear visible and Last Known contact.
- The effective leader should immediately resume the highest-priority mission objective instead of waiting stationary for formation.
- Supports should rebuild formation around that moving leader when practical.
- Repeat after leader loss/succession and with one unreachable support.

Casualty behavior
- Create a bleeding downed AEGIS casualty with a medkit-capable responder not initially adjacent.
- The responder should route toward and stabilize the casualty before escort/combat/source/rescue/search duties unless a hard override prevents action.
- A stabilized/downed casualty should draw a recovery/extraction responder before normal mission objectives.
- Ordinary non-bleeding medkit healing must not consume the responder before higher-priority mission actions; it is opportunistic after the main tactical pass.

Emergency / fallback behavior
- Exercise a planner failure while visible alien, Last Known Contact, Beacon/UFO responsibility, or known civilian assignment exists.
- The recovery planner must continue/hold the higher-priority objective; it must not silently fall back to generic patrol.

Hybrid / save continuity
- Hybrid explicit player movement remains player-owned unless an existing hard safety/escort rule says otherwise.
- Save and reload during contact, known-VIP approach, casualty recovery, and post-contact formation recovery.
- Save format remains 4 and objective state should resume without branching to exploration.
