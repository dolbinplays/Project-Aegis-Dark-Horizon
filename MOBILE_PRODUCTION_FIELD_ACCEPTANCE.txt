MOBILE RESEARCH / WORKSHOP — PHYSICAL DEVICE ACCEPTANCE
Build: v0.26.09.08.1739_MOBILE_RESEARCH_WORKSHOP_ADAPTIVE_LAYOUT_PATCH

Use a disposable campaign on physical phones/tablets and in the installed PWA.

1. Choose Mobile · Adaptive. In landscape, open Research and switch among Project, Topics, and Staff / Tech. Both command rails and section buttons should remain reachable while content scrolls.
2. Start an eligible topic. Confirm Project opens with the correct topic. Use minus/plus and the slider to assign scientists, including zero and the available maximum; progress and ETA should match.
3. Hire a scientist when capacity permits. Confirm the pending arrival and cost. Complete research in the disposable campaign and verify its technology unlock and ordinary campaign-day effects.
4. In Workshop, switch among Orders, Build, and Staff / Stock. Check all controls on a long order queue and confirm each section remembers its scroll position when switching away and back.
5. Adjust requested quantity and engineers using both steppers and sliders. Quantity cannot fall below completed items plus the current item. Engineers cannot exceed the staff available across orders.
6. Select a second production base, add an eligible order, and check its destination. Existing orders must retain their destination; stored items should follow the base selector in Staff / Stock.
7. Increase a queue beyond its funded quantity and advance days until the next item needs funding. Keep Queue Paused should leave it paused; approval should deduct one item's cost and resume it. Both choices must remain reachable above mobile controls.
8. Check an empty research project, empty build queue, and a base with no manufactured stock. Check selling one item updates only that base's stock.
9. Rotate portrait and landscape, expand/collapse browser chrome, and repeat on a tablet. No controls should extend horizontally beyond the central workspace; taller content should scroll.
10. Use Menu / Save to select Standard. Verify the original Research and Workshop panels and native sliders return, then switch back to Mobile.

Record device, OS, browser/PWA mode, viewport/orientation, and any touch, clipping, scrolling, or focus problems.
